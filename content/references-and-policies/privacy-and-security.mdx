# Privacy and Security

Your privacy is important to us. The **Claude to Word or PDF** Chrome extension is designed to work securely and respectfully with your data.

## No Content is Stored or Read

When you export a response or conversation, your content is processed strictly for the purpose of generating a Word or PDF file. The content is **never stored**, **never read by a human**, and **never used for any other purpose**.

- We do **not store** your Claude responses.
- We do **not review or analyze** the content in any way.
- The conversion process is automatic and temporary.

## No Personal Data Collected

We do **not collect** any personal information such as:

- Your name or email address
- Your Claude account information
- Your browsing history or activity

If you're on a paid plan, we store only the following, for license management:

- Your license key
- Basic usage counts

This is used solely to enforce plan limits and verify access.

## Secure by Design

- The extension only runs on Claude pages.
- It uses minimal permissions required for functionality.
- All processing is handled with privacy and security in mind.

## Permissions Explained

The extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

### Downloads
This allows the extension to automatically download the generated Word or PDF file to your device after conversion. No file is downloaded unless you initiate an export.

### Identity
These are used **only for paid users** to securely identify the logged-in Chrome profile. This helps us associate your license usage with your account while keeping your email and personal information private. We do not store your full email address.

### Storage
This is used to save your license key and preferences (such as export format) locally in your browser so that you don't have to re-enter them each time.

## You’re in Control

The extension acts only when you click the export buttons. You decide what to export and when.

You can remove the extension at any time via your browser’s extension settings.
