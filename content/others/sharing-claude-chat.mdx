# How to Share a Claude Conversation

You can easily share any Claude conversation using a public link. Here's how:

1. **Open the Chat**:   Navigate to the conversation you want to share in Claude.

2. **Click the Share Button**:  At the top of the chat window, you'll see a **Share** button . Click on it.

![Sharing Claude chat](/share-chat-button.png)

3. **Copy the Link**:  A dialog will appear with a preview of the conversation. Click the **Create link** button to create a sharable link. Once the link is created, it will automatically be copied to Clipboard.

![Sharing Claude chat](/share-chat-copy-link.png)


4. **Share the Link**: Paste the copied link anywhere — in an email, document, or message — to share the conversation with others.

> 💡 Anyone with the link will be able to view the shared conversation, but it will not include your name or any personal information.
