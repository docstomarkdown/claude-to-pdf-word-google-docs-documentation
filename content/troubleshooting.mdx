# Troubleshooting

If you're experiencing issues with the **Claude to Word or PDF** Chrome extension, here are some common problems and how to resolve them.

---

## Export buttons not showing

- **Make sure you’re on a Claude page**: The extension only activates on [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) chat pages (for example, `claude.ai/chat/...`).
- **Refresh the page**: After installing or updating the extension, reload the Claude tab.
- **Disable conflicting extensions**: Some extensions that alter Claude’s UI can interfere. Try disabling them temporarily.

---

## File not downloading

- **Check browser permissions**: Ensure that Chrome is allowed to download files. Navigate to Chrome settings → Privacy and security → Site settings → Automatic downloads.
- **Look for blocked popups**: Some browsers may block downloads automatically. Check your address bar for download warnings.
- **Try again**: Occasionally, a network or rendering delay can prevent the file from generating. Click the button again after a few seconds.

---

## License not recognized

- **Verify you entered the correct key**: Ensure there are no spaces or typos.
- **Refresh the Claude tab** after entering your license in the popup.
- **Still not working?** Contact us at [vikram@thinksolv.com](mailto:vikram@thinksolv.com) with your license key, and we’ll help you out. We typically respond to emails within 4 hours. Our time zone is IST (GMT +5:30).

---

## Monthly usage limit reached (Free plan)

- Free plan users are limited to a 10 single exports and 3 full conversation exports per month.
- Consider upgrading to a paid plan for higher limits and premium support.

---

## Extension not working in Microsoft Edge

- Microsoft Edge supports Chrome extensions. Visit the **Edge Add-ons Store**, or enable extension installation from the Chrome Web Store in your Edge settings.
- Once installed, the extension works the same as in Chrome.
