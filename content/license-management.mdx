# Manage License

The **Claude to PDF, Word and Google Docs** extension allows you to manage your license and upgrade to a premium plan for additional features. Below are the steps to activate, deactivate, and upgrade your license.

## Reaching the Free Limit

When you reach the **monthly limit** of the free plan (such as the number of exports available), you will see a **Quota Exceeded** message. At this point, you will no longer be able to export unless you upgrade to a paid plan.

- The message will offer two options:
  1. **Upgrade to a Paid Plan**: Click the **Buy Now** button to purchase any of the available plans.
  2. **Enter License Key**: If you already have a license, you can enter the license key to continue using the extension.

---

## Activating the License

Once you’ve purchased a plan, you will receive a **license key** via email. To activate your license and unlock unlimited conversions:

1. **Open the Claude window** where you are using the extension.
2. **Click the settings gear icon** in the top right corner of the window to open the settings menu.
   
  ![Screenshot of the quota exceeded message with upgrade and license key options](/manage-license.png)

3. In the settings menu, click on the **Manage License** option. This will open the **License Management** window.

4. Enter the **license key** you received via email in the provided field.
5. Click the **Activate** button to activate your license.

 ![Screenshot of license management window showing license key entry](/activate-license.png)

Once activated, the **unlimited conversions** feature will be unlocked, and you will no longer be subject to the monthly limits of the free plan.

## Using License on Multiple Devices

You can use one license across multiple devices provided that:

1. You are logged into the same Google account on all devices, and

2. [Chrome Sync](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/185277?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&oco=0#) is enabled.

The license key is synchronized through Chrome Sync. If Sync is turned off, the license will remain only on the device where it was activated and will not automatically transfer to other devices. In that case, the license effectively supports *only one activation (one device)*.

For security and abuse prevention reasons, license sharing across devices is supported only through Chrome Sync. Manual activation of the same license key on additional devices is not permitted, as there is no reliable way to verify whether multiple activations belong to the same user.

---

## Deactivating the License

To deactivate your license:

1. Open the **License Management** window again by clicking on the **Manage License** option in the settings.
2. In the License Management window, click on the **Deactivate** button.

   ![Screenshot of license management window showing deactivate button](/deactivate-license.png)

3. Once deactivated, your account will revert to the free plan, and you will be subject to the free plan limits.

---

> **Tip:** You can deactivate and reactivate your license at any time. Simply follow the activation steps again to re-enable unlimited conversions and remove the monthly export limits.

## Upgrading Plans

To upgrade your plan for yearly subscription:

1. Go to your [Billing Dashboard](https://thinksolv.lemonsqueezy.com/billing).

2. Choose a higher plan based on your needs.

Your new plan will take effect immediately.

## Cancelling Your Subscription

You can cancel your subscription anytime from the same [Billing Dashboard](https://thinksolv.lemonsqueezy.com/billing).

After cancellation:

Your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period.

After that, your account will revert to the free plan, which includes limited conversions per month.

No further charges will be made unless you reactivate your subscription.
