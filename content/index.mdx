# ChatGPT to Word or PDF Extension

Welcome to the official documentation for the **ChatGPT to Word or PDF** Chrome extension.

This extension allows you to seamlessly export ChatGPT responses into professionally formatted **Word (.docx)** or **PDF** files — perfect for saving research, client notes, lesson plans, documentation drafts, and more.

## Key Features

- **Export Individual Responses**  
  Download any single ChatGPT response as a Word or PDF file with one click.

- **Export Entire Conversations**  
  Convert full chats into clean, readable documents in your preferred format.

- **Support for Math and Formatting**  
  Equations, tables, bullet points, and markdown-style formatting are preserved beautifully — no extra editing needed.

- **Canvas Content Support**  
  Export content from ChatGPT Canvas mode

- **Deep Research Docs Export**  
  Works seamlessly with ChatGPT’s longer, structured research-style responses, preserving headings, sections, and citations in the exported document for sharing and archiving.
- **Free & Pro Plans**  
  Use the extension for free with usage limits, or upgrade for unlimited exports and additional features.

## Who Is It For?

This extension is ideal for:

- Students and researchers saving ChatGPT notes or summaries
- Professionals documenting AI-generated content
- Content creators and bloggers preparing drafts
- Anyone who wants to archive or share AI conversations
