# Exporting Claude Artifact Content

The **Claude to Word or PDF** extension also supports exporting content from **artifacts** — the side panel in Claude where long-form outputs are shown.

## How to Export Artifact Content

To export an artifact:

1. **Maximize the artifact** so that it is fully open on the screen.  
   (Note: The export only works when the artifact is open in full view.)

2. Once maximized, look at the **top right corner** of the artifact.

3. Click the **Word** or **PDF** button to export the artifact content in your desired format.

![Screenshot showing Word and PDF buttons for artifact export](/claude-artifact-export.png)

These buttons will behave differently based on the current view:

- If the **artifact is open**, clicking **Word/PDF** will export the **artifact content** only.
- If the **artifact is closed**, the same buttons will export the **entire conversation** instead.

## What Gets Exported

When exporting from an open artifact:

- Only the visible artifact content is exported.
- The structure, formatting, and math (if present) are preserved.

---

> **Tip:** Use this feature to export structured or visual outputs created as artifacts without capturing unrelated parts of the conversation.
