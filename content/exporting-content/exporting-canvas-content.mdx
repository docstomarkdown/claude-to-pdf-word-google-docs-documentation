# Exporting ChatGPT Canvas Content

The **ChatGPT to Word or PDF** extension also supports exporting content from **Canvas mode**, available in ChatGPT’s GPT-4 and team accounts.

## How to Export Canvas Content

To export a canvas:

1. **Maximize the canvas** so that it is fully open on the screen.  
   (Note: The export only works when the canvas is open in full view.)

2. Once maximized, look at the **top right corner** of the canvas.

3. Click the **Word** or **PDF** button to export the canvas content in your desired format.

![Screenshot showing Word and PDF buttons for canvas export](/chatgpt-canvas-export.png)

These buttons will behave differently based on the current view:

- If the **Canvas is open**, clicking **Word/PDF** will export the **Canvas content** only.
- If the **Canvas is closed**, the same buttons will export the **entire conversation** instead.

## What Gets Exported

When exporting from canvas mode:

- Only the visible canvas content is exported.
- The structure, formatting, and math (if present) are preserved.

---

> **Tip:** Use this feature to export structured or visual outputs created in Canvas mode without capturing unrelated parts of the conversation.
