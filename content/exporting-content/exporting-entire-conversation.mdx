# Exporting Entire Conversation

The **Claude to PDF, Word and Google Docs** extension lets you export the **entire conversation** in just one click. This includes the ability to:

- Export **both user prompts and Claude responses**  
- Or export **only the Claude responses**, omitting the user input

## Exporting Entire Conversation with User Prompts

1. Open any Claude conversation at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai).
2. Look at the **top right corner** of the conversation window.
3. Click the **Word**, **PDF**, or **Google Docs** button to export the entire conversation.

![Screenshot showing the top right export buttons for the full conversation](/chatgpt-entire-conversation-export.png)

By default, both user prompts and responses will be included.

---

## Export Only Claude Responses from Entire Conversation

If you prefer to export **only the Claude responses**, follow these steps:

1. Click the **Settings gear icon** in the top right corner of the Claude conversation page.  
2. In the **Settings** popup, scroll to the **Conversion Settings** section.
3. Enable the checkbox labeled **"Export only Claude responses."**

![Screenshot showing the Export only Claude responses checkbox in Settings](/chatgpt-only-chatgpt-responses.png)

> **Note:** Once enabled, this setting will be **saved automatically** and used as the default for future exports.  
You can disable it anytime by returning to the same Settings window.

---

> **Tip:** Use this option when you only want to archive or share the AI's output without including your prompts or instructions.
