# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General

### What does the ChatGPT to Word or PDF extension do?

This extension allows you to convert your ChatGPT conversations into downloadable Word or PDF files. It supports formatting, math equations, and even support exporting of entire conversations.

### How do I use the extension?

1. Install the extension from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/chatgpt-to-word-or-pdf/mjdmggegbkookpcmbdllcnbfboikcbop).
2. Open a ChatGPT conversation.
3. Click the **"Word"** or **"PDF"** button below any response or on the top bar to download the conversation in your preferred format.

### Can I export multiple responses or entire conversations?

Yes, you can export:

* A **single response** using the button below each response.
* An **entire conversation** using the button on the top right.

### Are math equations supported?

Yes! Equations and symbols are converted seamlessly, especially into Word documents—no post-editing required.

You won’t lose any of the Word or PDF files you’ve already downloaded—those are saved to your device and fully owned by you. After cancellation, your account will automatically switch to the free plan.

Yes, one license works across devices as long as you're logged into the same Chrome account.

## Troubleshooting

### The export button is not showing up. What should I do?

* Make sure you're on the ChatGPT website (chat.openai.com).
* Ensure you're logged in.
* Refresh the page or restart the browser.
* If it still doesn't work, [contact support](mailto:support@thinksolv.com).

### The formatting looks off. How can I fix it?

Formatting should be consistent with the chat, but if you find issues:

* Try exporting in the other format (Word or PDF).
* Report the bug to us with screenshots and details.
